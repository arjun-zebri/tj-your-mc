# Voice examples

Before and after pairs, all taken from the live old site. Read these when you are
unsure what the voice sounds like in practice.

---

**Old, homepage hero**

> Renowned for his charisma, professionalism, and ability to captivate any
> audience with his voice, TJ has earned the title of Sydney's most sought-after
> professional MC in Sydney.

What is wrong: three unbackable superlatives, an invented title, "in Sydney"
twice, written in third person about a one-man business, and it tells you nothing
about what he actually does.

**Direction**

> I run the part of your wedding where everything actually happens. The
> introductions, the speeches, the bit where two hundred people need to know
> where to go next.

---

**Old, service section**

> As a professional event MC in Sydney, my goal is to connect your guests with
> the flawless day, ensuring the party runs smoothly and concludes on a
> distinctive note.

What is wrong: "flawless", "distinctive note", and the sentence does not quite
parse. It sounds translated.

**Direction**

> My job is to keep the night moving. Guests know where to be, speeches start and
> finish, and nobody is standing around wondering what happens next.

---

**Old, about page**

> I'm not just any civil celebrant, and thus, make myself exceptional by pouring
> flawlessness into your wedding experience.

What is wrong: factually incorrect, TJ is not a celebrant. Also "thus", also
"pouring flawlessness", also "experience".

**Direction**

> I am not the celebrant. She handles the legal bit. I take over once everyone
> sits down for dinner, which is where most weddings either fly or drag.

---

**Old, about page, the good bit**

> - I'm not boring or cheesy
> - I don't overdo it, but put a big, long performance
> - I don't steal the limelight

This is the most human thing on the old site and it clearly came from TJ rather
than the agency. Keep the substance, fix the second one, which is garbled.

**Direction**

> - I keep it warm without doing bits
> - I introduce you, then get out of the way
> - When the running order slips, your guests never find out

Three negatives in a row reads as defensive, and the reader has to translate each
one into what he actually does. Say the doing thing.

---

**Old, call to action**

> Get A Quote Now

What is wrong: title case, generic, and it describes a transaction rather than
what the person actually wants to know.

**Direction**

> Check my date

---

## The test

Every line above passes the same test. Swap in a competitor's name and the old
version still works perfectly, because it says nothing. The new version does not,
because it says something specific about how this particular bloke works.
